The Methodology

A structured, data-driven approach to building sequences that convert finance leaders into demo bookings.

01

Discovery & ICP Mapping

Day 1–2

Quick-start audit of your current HubSpot setup, ICP definitions, and Qashio's core value props. Map pain points to each target persona so every email hits the right nerve.

02

Segmentation & Sequence Architecture

Day 2–3

Define audience segments by role, company size, and industry. Map out the flow, cadence, and branching logic for all 3 sequences before writing a single word.

03

Email Copywriting & A/B Variants

Empathy + Specificity

Day 3
Cost of Inaction
Subject: The hidden cost of 'good enough' expense tools
Quantify the cost of status quo — lost hours, compliance risk, zero spend visibility. Position Qashio as the modern alternative.
→ Value-driven CTA — 'See the ROI calculator in action'
Day 5
Social Proof
Subject: How [Similar Company] cut expense processing by 80%
Social proof lead. Reference Qashio's real customer wins — FITT Meals, Rmal, etc. Make the result feel tangible and achievable.

Engagement Trigger

Consultative angle. Ask about their current card/expense stack. Position as helpful, not salesy. Opens a reply thread.

Day 12
Unique Value Prop
Subject: Last thing — the rewards angle your CFO will love
Differentiation play. Lead with Emirates Skywards Miles, cashback, Shukran rewards. Qashio's unique moat. No competitor matches this.
→ Final push — 'See the full rewards breakdown in a quick demo'
